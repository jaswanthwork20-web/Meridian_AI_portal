# Uploading client tax documents

**Confluence URL:** https://agnishpaul2002.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SELFHELP/pages/2916354
**Page ID:** 2916354

## Content

Overview Each client may have specific tax documentation on file, such as a tax exemption certificate or GST registration certificate for their jurisdiction. Steps Select the client before uploading their document - documents are tied to the specific client, not the invoice. Upload the relevant certificate in the Tax Document field on the Raise Invoice screen. If you switch to a different client, you must upload that client's own document - a previous client's file does not carry over. Note Missing or mismatched tax documentation may delay invoice approval.
