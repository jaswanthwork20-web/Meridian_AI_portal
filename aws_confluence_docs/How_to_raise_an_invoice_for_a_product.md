# How to raise an invoice for a product

**Confluence URL:** https://agnishpaul2002.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SELFHELP/pages/1441793
**Page ID:** 1441793

## Content

Overview This article explains the standard steps to raise an invoice for any product already configured in the system. Steps Go to Invoices > Raise Invoice. Select the product, enter quantity, and choose the correct tax code. Add any remarks if applicable, then click Submit. Confirm the invoice appears under 'My Invoices' with status 'Submitted'.
