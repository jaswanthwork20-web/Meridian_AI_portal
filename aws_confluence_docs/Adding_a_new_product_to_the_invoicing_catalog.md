# Adding a new product to the invoicing catalog

**Confluence URL:** https://agnishpaul2002.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/SELFHELP/pages/1343730
**Page ID:** 1343730

## Content

Overview This article describes the general request process for onboarding a new product into the invoicing catalog. Steps Submit a product onboarding request with product name, category, and applicable tax code. The Configuration team reviews and adds the product to the master data table. Allow up to 2 business days for the product to appear across all invoicing screens. Note If a newly added product does not appear where expected after this window, raise a support ticket rather than resubmitting the request.
